The Vision You Support

🌟 Our Vision Statement

To be a light to the nations by taking the Gospel to every village and community through mission crusades, reaching hospitals with evangelism, medical outreach, and financial support for patients’ bills; feeding the hungry, clothing the naked, and distributing Bibles, devotionals, and relief materials, so that the sick are healed, the poor are loved, and countless souls are won into the Kingdom of Jesus Christ.

🔥 Our Mission

Cedar Light Evangelical Ministry exists to take the Gospel to the nations through mission crusades and hospital evangelism, while demonstrating Christ’s love through medical outreach, paying hospital bills, feeding the hungry, clothing the naked, and distributing Bibles, devotionals, and relief materials — to heal the sick, meet practical needs, and win souls for the Kingdom of Jesus Christ.

Our Core Values
1. Christ-Centered Evangelism Mark 16:15 — We go because souls matter. Preaching in villages, cities & hospitals is our first priority.
2. Compassion in Action Matt 25:35-36 — We feed the hungry, clothe the naked, pay hospital bills, and meet real needs.
3. Prayer & Healing Faith James 5:14-15 — We believe God still heals. We pray for the sick and trust God for miracles.
4. Global Missions Mindset Acts 1:8 — From our community to the ends of the earth. Every village is our mission field.
5. Integrity & Stewardship Proverbs 11:1 — We handle people, resources, and finances with honesty and transparency unto God.
6. Discipleship & Equipping Matt 28:19-20 — We grow disciples by distributing Bibles, devotionals & teaching believers.
